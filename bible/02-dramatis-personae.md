# Campaign Bible — Dramatis Personae

*The Real Work*

Authoritative roster for names, spellings, and continuity. Curated by Matt. Add people as the
table meets them; move them to **Adversaries** or mark them dead as it happens.

## The Company

- **Trivius Malrec** *(player: ?)* — tiefling transmuter. Bio TBD.
- **Wende Sandhauler** *(player: ?)* — quarry dwarf, mid-thirties. Copper-red braids bound with
  dull steel rings and grey stone dust worked into them; a broad once-broken nose; no beard. A
  battered chain shirt over a padded coat, a plain steel shield, a warhammer.
- **Esper Toevel** *(player: ?)* — human bard, late twenties, lean and quick-faced. Deep green
  wool coat with tarnished brass buttons, a wound scarf, a small brass-fitted stringed instrument
  carried high against the chest. Always about to say something.
- **Jules Arine** *(player: ?)* — human priestess of Shelyn, early thirties. Warm brown skin, dark
  hair pinned up with strands escaping, a calm level face that is not sweet. Dove-grey and rose
  robes over a gambeson; a silver-and-enamel songbird on a chain, her only ornament; a glaive
  across her back.
- **Dorogh Kell** *(player: ?)* — half-orc fighter, early twenties, noticeably bigger than a
  human. Grey-green skin, subtly pointed ears, two small clean lower tusks, an open nasal helm, and
  plain round wire spectacles. Quick-minded, and making a careful, visible effort to look friendly.
- **Diddle Scribes** *(player: ?)* — goblin alchemist, roughly fifteen years out of the ditch
  James Helmsmith found him in. Small even for a goblin and leaner than his kin; huge tapered ears,
  four small yellow eyes set in the dark under a deep hood; a quilted tan coat over a long brown
  work-coat hung with pouches and a belt of stoppered vials. Reared by a human shopkeeper and
  taught to trade with the other races — rough going at first, easier once Drezen got used to him.
  Quirky and a touch naive; poor at people, excellent at potions. Signed on with the town guard to
  see the world. **James Helmsmith** — his adoptive father, human, alchemist and shopkeeper, once
  an adventurer who gathered his own reagents.

## Employers

- **Mira Thistledance** — half-elf bard, Lupenor Celest's cohort; keeps Lupenor's Market and
  screens every trader who wants a stall. Hires people with no company behind them for work she
  does not want traced to the Market, and does not apologise for it.
- **Elara Dawnstrider** — human paladin of Iomedae, who renounced her noble family; calls the
  recruits of Iomedae's Preservers and runs the order's three-day trials. Holds Drezen's order
  while Harlock is in the west.

## Known in Drezen

People the crusade's chronicle has established in the city, whom the company may meet. Their
full entries are in The Fifth Crusade's Cast; the one-line versions here are all this table needs.

- **Irabeth Tirabade** — half-orc paladin, Governor of liberated Drezen.
- **Aron Kir** — former spy, Warden of Drezen; keeps the citadel's watch and its secrets.
- **Sosiel Vaenic** — priest of Shelyn; keeps the city's temple of the united faiths. Jules's
  co-religionist.
- **Horgus Gwerm** — the city's blunt, unsentimental quartermaster.
- **Rothin Vald** — priest of Sarenrae; keeper of the Hidden Temple of Sarenrae two days west.
- **Joran Vhane** — dwarven master smith, a prisoner turned penitent, dreaming of relighting the
  forge beneath Drezen.
