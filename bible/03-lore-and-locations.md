# Campaign Bible — Lore, Locations & Artifacts

*The Real Work*

## The situation

**Drezen** was retaken by the crusade on the **13th of Neth, 4713 AR**, after seventy years under
demon rule. The crusade holds the walls and the citadel. It does not yet hold every quarter, and
the city is still learning how to be a city again — a garrison, a market, two temples, a
quartermaster browbeating the crown for grain, and a great many buildings nobody has searched.

The mythic four — **Harlock Greyforge**, **Lupenor Celest**, **Rabiah** and **Varic Sarian** —
are away in the west, days out of contact. The cohorts they left behind are holding the city and
the road between them on a thread. That is where the company comes in.

## Places

- **Drezen** — the liberated citadel-city on the Ahari; the company's base.
- **Lupenor's Market** — Lupenor Celest's foundation in Drezen; Mira Thistledance screens its
  traders. The company's first employer keeps an office here.
- **Iomedae's Preservers** — Harlock's order: five squads of paladins and clerics sworn to hold
  Drezen. Elara Dawnstrider calls its recruits.
- **The temple of Sarenrae** and **the temple of the united faiths** — across the way from each
  other; Varic Sarian's foundation and Sosiel Vaenic's.
- **The South Bank** — a quarter of scrap yards and rope-walks that spent seventy years under
  demon rule and has not been searched. **Cinder Row** runs through it.
- **The western road** — two days on foot from Drezen to the Fane, with nothing on it.
- **The Hidden Temple of Sarenrae** (the Sarian Fane) — Rothin Vald's outpost two days west.
- **The Marchlands** — the broken country west of Drezen. The crusade's chart of it is the Map
  tab on The Fifth Crusade's site.

Add places as the company finds them, and put the ones worth a pin into `maps/`.

## The world's own reckoning

Golarion's calendar: twelve months of Gregorian length (Abadius through Kuthona), a seven-day week
(Moon Day, Toil Day, Weal Day, Oath Day, Fire Day, Star Day, Sun Day). Year 4713 AR. The weekday
anchor is shared with the crusade's chronicle — see `build.ps1`.
