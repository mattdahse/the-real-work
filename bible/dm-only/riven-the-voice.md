# DM-only — Riven Maelus and the voice at Threshold

*From John's background email of 13 September 2026. **Riven has told no one in the party.**
This file is tracked, but nothing in `bible/dm-only/` is ever compiled into `data.js` — the build
reads only `source/`, `secrets/`, `maps/` and the calendar — so it does not appear on the site.
The repository itself is public. Matt's call, deliberately: the story point matters more than the
chance a player goes looking, and the table does not play on out-of-character knowledge.
**Do not add this directory to `secrets/`** — that folder is in-world documents and it does render.*

At **Threshold**, Riven found old documentation on transmutation and tried a small, harmless
working. It turned on his own body instead and left him physically stronger than before. That
much is in the public bio, and Riven will admit to it.

What is not: immediately afterwards, **he heard a voice.** He does not know where it came from.
It told him he had succeeded, and gave him one instruction —

> "Open another Worldwound."

It named **Mendev**.

Riven has no idea whether it was a warning, a command, a temptation, a trick, or something else;
who or what spoke; why him; or what opening another Worldwound would actually accomplish. John was
explicit that Riven is **not** secretly important, bloodline-descended, or carrying a destiny he
already understands — the voice is an unexplained event that happened to an ordinary first-level
wanderer, and the answer is Matt's to invent.

## Hooks John offered

- **Vaelus and Seraphine Maelus** — the missing parents. Fate deliberately unknown.
- **Blackreed** — the settlement, and the crusader raid that took them from him.
- **The crusaders in that raid** — Riven remembers some of the people involved, incompletely.
  Drezen is full of crusaders.
- **Threshold** — unplaced, and open for Matt to site.
- **The voice** — no explanation, no guarantee it is trustworthy.
- **Opening another Worldwound** — he knows the instruction, not the reason.

John: *"Use what you like, where it fits."*

## Mechanical note from the same email

John expects to adjust skills. Riven is **not** broadly studied — knowledges are meant to grow in
play rather than start spread across the board. He wants **Survival**, **Sense Motive**, and
something for **repairing things** (Craft, or Profession if the table would rather).
