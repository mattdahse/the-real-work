# Drezen

*The liberated citadel-city on the Ahari — the company's base, and the ground of the real work*

<!-- image: images/map-drezen-city.webp -->
<!-- order: 1 -->
<!-- scale: the walls to the South Bank — a little under a mile -->

Seventy years of demon rule, and then the walls came back into crusade hands on the **13th of
Neth, 4713**. What the chart shows is a city relearning how to be one: the citadel and its
curtain wall holding the high ground, two compounds gone up on the eastern ridge, a market
crowded against the gate, and a whole quarter across the dry Ahari that nobody has searched.
North is to the upper right.

## Citadel Drezen
<!-- at: 38.6, 29.8 -->
<!-- letter: A -->
<!-- kind: city -->

The star-fort in its moat, inside a pentagonal curtain wall with six towers and one gate facing
south into the city. The crusade holds it outright. Everything the company is paid to do is
counted out of this arch and counted back through it.

## Iomedae's Preservers
<!-- at: 74.8, 16.2 -->
<!-- letter: B -->
<!-- kind: city -->

**Harlock Greyforge's** order, walled on the high ground north-east of the city: garrison,
barracks, chapel, stable, a watchtower over the western approach, and a parade ground that is
still drilled every morning. **Elara Dawnstrider** calls its recruits. Five squads to hold a
city, and their commander is days out of contact in the west.

## Varic's Temple Compound
<!-- at: 66.4, 37.2 -->
<!-- letter: C -->
<!-- kind: temple -->

**Varic Sarian's** foundation on the eastern ridge, ringed by its own low wall. The **Temple of
Sarenrae** stands at the centre with the **temple of the united faiths** — **Sosiel Vaenic's** —
across the way from it. An orphanage, a food bank and an infirmary fill the rest of the ground,
which says plainly what the compound is actually for.

## Rabiah's Compound
<!-- at: 80.8, 38.3 -->
<!-- letter: D -->
<!-- kind: site -->

The ring quarter east of the ridge: bazaar, brewery, bathhouse, community kitchen, workshop row
and a shrine over a meeting square, wound in circles around the old amphitheatre. Built fast and
built crooked, and the most alive ground in Drezen.

## The Amphitheatre
<!-- at: 81.1, 33.8 -->
<!-- kind: ruin -->
<!-- style: icon -->

Pre-crusade stone at the heart of Rabiah's quarter, cleared and back in use. The one place in
the city that will hold a crowd.

## Celeste Market District
<!-- at: 52.1, 54.2 -->
<!-- letter: E -->
<!-- kind: site -->

**Lupenor Celest's** foundation, packed between the citadel wall and the riverbank: street
vendors under canvas, an auction house, warehouses along the wall, three inns and
**Lupenor's residence** above it all. **Mira Thistledance** screens the traders who work here,
and the company's first employer keeps an office on this ground.

## The Ahari Riverbed
<!-- at: 44.9, 65.4 -->
<!-- kind: water -->

The river ran out of its bed when the Worldwound opened and never came back. What is left is a
dry channel cutting the city in half, crossed by two bridges — and a road for anything that
would rather not use the gates.

## South Bank
<!-- at: 53.9, 81.8 -->
<!-- letter: F -->
<!-- kind: ruin -->

The quarter across the riverbed: scrap yards and rope-walks that spent seventy years under demon
rule and have not been searched. **Cinder Row** runs through it. The crusade holds the walls
above it and very little of what is under its roofs.

## The Cemetery
<!-- at: 7.2, 75.7 -->
<!-- kind: site -->
<!-- style: icon -->

Outside the walls to the west, on the far bank. Older than the occupation, and added to steadily
since the retaking.

## The Western Road
<!-- at: 3.0, 46.0 -->
<!-- kind: region -->

Out of the west gate and into the Marchlands: two days on foot to the **Sarian Fane**, with
nothing on the road in between.
