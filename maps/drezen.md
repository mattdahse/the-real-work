# Drezen

*The liberated citadel-city on the Ahari. The company's base, and the ground of the real work.*

<!-- image: images/map-drezen-city.webp -->
<!-- order: 1 -->
<!-- scale: the walls to the South Bank, a little under a mile -->

Seventy-five years of demon rule, and then the walls came back into crusade hands on the **13th
of Rova, 4713**. What the chart shows is a city relearning how to be one: the citadel and its
curtain wall holding the high ground, two compounds gone up on the eastern ridge, a market
crowded against the gate, and a whole quarter across the dry Ahari that nobody has searched,
because searching it would mean going in. North is to the upper right. The cemetery is to the
left, and growing.

## Citadel Drezen
<!-- at: 38.6, 29.8 -->
<!-- letter: A -->
<!-- kind: city -->

The star-fort in its moat, inside a pentagonal curtain wall with six towers and one gate facing
south into the city. The crusade holds it outright, and the garrison lives here, behind the
walls, where the crusade has always preferred to be. Everything the company is paid to do is
counted out of this arch and counted back through it. The ledger at the gate has three columns:
*Out*, *In*, and *Shrinkage*. It is the most complete record Drezen keeps.

## Iomedae's Preservers
<!-- at: 74.8, 16.2 -->
<!-- letter: B -->
<!-- kind: city -->

**Harlock Greyforge's** order, walled on the high ground north-east of the city: garrison,
barracks, chapel, stable, a watchtower over the western approach, and a parade ground drilled
every morning by people who are not yet sure what they are drilling for. Five squads to hold a
city, and their commander days out of contact in the west. **Elara Dawnstrider** calls the
recruits and runs the three-day trial. Her account of it: "The first day tells me if you can
stand. The second tells me if you can stand it. The third is for the ones I'm keeping, so they
can eat."

## Varic's Temple Compound
<!-- at: 66.4, 37.2 -->
<!-- letter: C -->
<!-- kind: temple -->

**Varic Sarian's** foundation on the eastern ridge, ringed by its own low wall, because even the
Dawnflower's people have noticed where they are. The **Temple of Sarenrae** stands at the centre
with the **temple of the united faiths**, **Sosiel Vaenic's**, across the way from it. Asked how
the gods were getting along under one roof, Sosiel said, "Better than their priests. I've had to
move the pews twice." An orphanage, a food bank and an infirmary fill the rest of the ground,
which says plainly what the compound is for, and what the city is producing.

## Rabiah's Compound
<!-- at: 80.8, 38.3 -->
<!-- letter: D -->
<!-- kind: site -->

The ring quarter east of the ridge: bazaar, brewery, bathhouse, community kitchen, workshop row
and a shrine over a meeting square, wound in circles around the old amphitheatre. Built fast and
built crooked. The kitchen and the dining hall were begun from two ends by two people and met at
the wrong angle, and the result is known throughout Drezen as **Tuesday's mistake**. Wednesday,
they started over there. **Tilda Merrow**, an aging adept, keeps the compound from dying of its
own enthusiasm, and describes her duties as "splints, soup, and saying no." **Sir Brannic
Vayne** keeps the lists. He is not really a knight. He has beautiful handwriting, and a seal, and
has never confirmed or denied which came first.

## The Amphitheatre
<!-- at: 81.1, 33.8 -->
<!-- kind: ruin -->
<!-- style: icon -->

Pre-crusade stone at the heart of Rabiah's quarter, cleared and back in use. The one place in the
city that will hold a crowd, which nobody has yet had a reason to test.

## Celeste Market District
<!-- at: 52.1, 54.2 -->
<!-- letter: E -->
<!-- kind: site -->

**Lupenor Celest's** foundation, packed between the citadel wall and the riverbank: street
vendors under canvas, an auction house, warehouses along the wall, three inns and **Lupenor's
residence** above it all, where the money can be watched. **Mira Thistledance** screens the
traders who work here. Asked her criteria, she said, "Yes." **Silas Thorne** sells cloth, in a
city where everyone's clothes are ruined, and is doing very well. **Abner Suthi** trades honey
and sweets; he once died on a spike above a lava lake and was brought back, and he sells sweets,
and he has thought about it. From the board outside the auction house, this week:

> *ROPE, new, Kenabres: 4 sp the fathom.*\
> *ROPE, used, South Bank: ask.*\
> *HONEY, Suthi's: 2 sp the jar. Sweets: 1 cp, or a story he hasn't heard.*\
> *CLOTH, Thorne's: whatever you have. He will make it work.*\
> *WANTED: persons of no company for a job of no description. Office above. Knock once.*

The company's first employer keeps that office.

## The Ahari Riverbed
<!-- at: 44.9, 65.4 -->
<!-- kind: water -->

The river ran out of its bed when the Worldwound opened and never came back. What is left is a
dry channel cutting the city in half, crossed by two bridges, and a road for anything that would
rather not use the gates. A great many things would rather not.

## South Bank
<!-- at: 53.9, 81.8 -->
<!-- letter: F -->
<!-- kind: ruin -->

The quarter across the riverbed: scrap yards and rope-walks that spent seventy-five years under
demon rule and have not been searched. **Cinder Row** runs through it. The crusade holds the
walls above it and very little of what is under its roofs, and has decided that this counts.
This is where the work is. This is why the company is being paid, and why the pay is what it is.

## The Cemetery
<!-- at: 7.2, 75.7 -->
<!-- kind: site -->
<!-- style: icon -->

Outside the walls to the west, on the far bank. Older than the occupation, and added to steadily
since the retaking. The only district in Drezen with a growing population.

## The Western Road
<!-- at: 3.0, 46.0 -->
<!-- kind: region -->

Out of the west gate and into the Marchlands: two days on foot to the **Sarian Fane**, with
nothing on the road in between. "Nothing" is a technical term.
